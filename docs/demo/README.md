The 110-second narrated cut uses validated 1920×1080 product footage and covers:

1. the document-to-action problem and LocalGuard's local evidence boundary;
2. local document evidence and immutable revisions;
3. a question across the indexed evidence vault;
4. an evidence-grounded answer with exact citation proof;
5. an explicitly synthetic action scenario and the human approval boundary;
6. exactly-once internal task creation and its audit trail; and
7. a concise architecture summary.

The reviewer signs in before recording starts. The video contains no password, bearer token, or
ignored `.env` value. All visible source documents and workflow records are synthetic demonstration
data. Long local-model waits are shortened in the edit; the displayed states come from the real
browser journey.

The separate [twelve-step pipeline walkthrough](../pipeline.md) includes the latest stored,
verified 25/25 local-model evaluation. That evaluation completed on August 24, 2026 and is not
presented as a run performed during the August 30 screenshot or video capture.
