[LG-POL-007:H01] 1. Standard changes
[LG-POL-007:P001]
[LG-POL-007:L001] A Change Owner must submit a standard production change at least two business days before the requested deployment window.
[LG-POL-007:L002] Every production change must include a tested rollback plan and evidence from a non-production environment.
[LG-POL-007:L003] A high-risk production change requires Service Owner approval before scheduling.

[LG-POL-007:H02] 2. Deployment windows
[LG-POL-007:P002]
[LG-POL-007:L004] Routine production deployments are permitted on Tuesday and Thursday from 18:00 to 20:00 local time.
[LG-POL-007:L005] The Release Coordinator records the start time, completion time, outcome, and rollback decision.

[LG-POL-007:H03] 3. Emergency changes
[LG-POL-007:P003]
[LG-POL-007:L006] An emergency change may proceed outside the routine window when the Duty Manager records the operational reason.
[LG-POL-007:L007] The Change Owner must submit an emergency-change retrospective within one business day after deployment.
[LG-POL-007:L008] The retrospective must identify testing performed, observed impact, and any follow-up work.
