[LG-POL-001:H01] 1. Scope and ownership
[LG-POL-001:P001]
[LG-POL-001:L001] This standard governs temporary system access for external vendors at the LocalGuard Demonstration Organization.
[LG-POL-001:L002] Vendor Operations owns the onboarding record, while the Department Sponsor remains accountable for business need and timely offboarding.

[LG-POL-001:H02] 2. Pre-access request
[LG-POL-001:P002]
[LG-POL-001:L003] The Department Sponsor must submit a complete Vendor Access Form at least seven business days before the vendor start date.
[LG-POL-001:L004] Vendor Operations must confirm completeness within one business day after receiving the form.
[LG-POL-001:L005] The Security Review Team must approve or return a complete access request within two business days after the completeness confirmation.

[LG-POL-001:H03] 3. High-risk vendors
[LG-POL-001:P003]
[LG-POL-001:L006] Before credentials are issued to a high-risk vendor, the Department Sponsor must provide both a signed data-processing addendum and a completed security questionnaire.
[LG-POL-001:L007] Shared accounts are prohibited; each approved vendor user receives an individually attributable account.

[LG-POL-001:H04] 4. Expiry and offboarding
[LG-POL-001:P004]
[LG-POL-001:L008] Vendor access expires after ninety calendar days unless the Department Sponsor submits an approved renewal before expiry.
[LG-POL-001:L009] The Department Sponsor must notify the Service Desk within four hours after a vendor offboarding decision.
[LG-POL-001:L010] The Service Desk must disable the vendor account within one hour after receiving the offboarding notice.
