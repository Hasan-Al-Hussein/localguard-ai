[LG-POL-008:H01] 1. Assignment
[LG-POL-008:P001]
[LG-POL-008:L001] The Asset Custodian must acknowledge assigned remote equipment within one business day after collection.
[LG-POL-008:L002] The acknowledgement must list the asset tag, equipment type, condition, and issue date.
[LG-POL-008:L003] Organizational accounts must not be shared with another household member or worker.

[LG-POL-008:H02] 2. Loss and protection
[LG-POL-008:P002]
[LG-POL-008:L004] The Asset Custodian must report lost equipment to the Service Desk within thirty minutes after discovery.
[LG-POL-008:L005] The Service Desk must notify the Security Team within one hour after receiving a loss report.
[LG-POL-008:L006] Sensitive organizational files may be stored only on encrypted organizational storage.

[LG-POL-008:H03] 3. Return and inventory
[LG-POL-008:P003]
[LG-POL-008:L007] The Asset Custodian must return assigned equipment within three business days after the offboarding date.
[LG-POL-008:L008] The Asset Manager must reconcile the remote-equipment inventory once every calendar quarter.
[LG-POL-008:L009] Unresolved inventory differences must be assigned to an accountable owner within two business days.
