[LG-POL-004:H01] 1. Retention periods
[LG-POL-004:P001]
[LG-POL-004:L001] System access logs must be retained for twelve months after the recorded activity.
[LG-POL-004:L002] Vendor due-diligence records must be retained for thirty-six months after the related vendor agreement terminates.
[LG-POL-004:L003] Incident evidence must be retained for twenty-four months after incident closure.
[LG-POL-004:L004] Facility inspection records must be retained for three years after the inspection date.

[LG-POL-004:H02] 2. Disposal control
[LG-POL-004:P002]
[LG-POL-004:L005] The Records Officer reviews eligible records on the first business day of each month.
[LG-POL-004:L006] The responsible system owner must complete approved deletion within ten business days after receiving a disposal notice.
[LG-POL-004:L007] The system owner must record the deletion date, record class, and verification method in the disposal register.

[LG-POL-004:H03] 3. Holds
[LG-POL-004:P003]
[LG-POL-004:L008] An active legal or investigation hold suspends normal disposal until the Records Officer records a written release.
[LG-POL-004:L009] A hold changes the disposal date but does not change the record owner.
