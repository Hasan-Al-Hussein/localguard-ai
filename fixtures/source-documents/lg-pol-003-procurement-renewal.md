[LG-POL-003:H01] 1. Purchase approval
[LG-POL-003:P001]
[LG-POL-003:L001] A purchase above twenty-five thousand internal credits requires Procurement Lead approval before an order is placed.
[LG-POL-003:L002] The request owner must attach at least two comparable quotations to a purchase above twenty-five thousand internal credits.
[LG-POL-003:L003] Legal review is required before signing an agreement that permits a vendor to process organizational data.

[LG-POL-003:H02] 2. Renewal planning
[LG-POL-003:P002]
[LG-POL-003:L004] The Contract Owner must submit a renewal review forty-five calendar days before the renewal date.
[LG-POL-003:L005] The renewal review must record service performance, unresolved risks, expected cost, and the recommendation to renew or exit.
[LG-POL-003:L006] For an auto-renewing agreement, Procurement must send any cancellation notice at least thirty calendar days before the renewal date.

[LG-POL-003:H03] 3. Invoice variance
[LG-POL-003:P003]
[LG-POL-003:L007] Accounts Payable may process an invoice variance up to five percent when the Contract Owner records a reason.
[LG-POL-003:L008] A variance above five percent requires a corrected invoice or written Procurement Lead approval.
