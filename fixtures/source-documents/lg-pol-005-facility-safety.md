[LG-POL-005:H01] 1. Daily inspection
[LG-POL-005:P001]
[LG-POL-005:L001] The Shift Supervisor must complete the pre-opening safety checklist by 08:30 local time on every operating day.
[LG-POL-005:L002] The checklist covers exits, fire equipment, floor hazards, emergency lighting, and first-aid supplies.

[LG-POL-005:H02] 2. Critical hazards
[LG-POL-005:P002]
[LG-POL-005:L003] A worker who identifies a critical hazard must isolate the affected area immediately when it is safe to do so.
[LG-POL-005:L004] The worker must notify the Safety Coordinator within ten minutes after identifying the critical hazard.
[LG-POL-005:L005] Only the Safety Coordinator may authorize reopening an isolated area.

[LG-POL-005:H03] 3. Corrective actions
[LG-POL-005:P003]
[LG-POL-005:L006] A Priority 1 corrective action is due within twenty-four hours after it is assigned.
[LG-POL-005:L007] A Priority 2 corrective action is due within five business days after it is assigned.
[LG-POL-005:L008] The Shift Supervisor must attach completion evidence before marking a corrective action closed.
