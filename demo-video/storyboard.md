## Visual system

- Obsidian `#07121C`, ink navy `#0A3048`, proof mint `#52E0C4`, ion blue `#4F8CFF`,
  human-gate amber `#F2A93B`, mineral silver `#DCE6E8`.
- Manrope for display copy, Geist for interface copy, monospace only for IDs and hashes.
- Motion uses short ease-out reveals and straight evidence travel; no playful bounce, particle storm,
  or decorative 3D object unrelated to the pipeline.
- Captions stay inside an 80 px horizontal and 100 px vertical safe area.
- Product recordings remain readable at normal speed; waiting periods are cut rather than falsified.
